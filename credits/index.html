>>> QUIRKS <<<

The films are links, not embedded players. A YouTube embed sets cookies and reports you to Google the moment the page loads, whether or not you press play. Nothing on this site loads from anyone else's server until you choose to go there.

The counter is counting you, not everyone. It is a number kept in your own browser. No server ever hears about it — which is why it starts at one, and why your phone and your laptop will disagree.

Everything moving here stops if your system asks it to. The ticker, the sparkles, the barricades: all of it honours prefers-reduced-motion.
