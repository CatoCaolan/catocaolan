>>> WHAT THIS IS <<<

Eight pieces, 2015 to 2023. Seven of them made with CinemaRaven, the production company I worked at as assistant director from 2014 to 2018 — sketch comedy, a music video, a 48 Hour Film Festival entry, and the company reel.

The roles move around a lot, and that is the honest picture of small-crew film work: you write it, then you are in it, then you are holding the boom for someone else's. Every credit below is listed as it actually was.
