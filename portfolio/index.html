>>> GAMES <<<

Thirteen games, designed, built and shipped end to end with an agent fleet and no coding background when I started. They live inside MapChat, on iOS and Android.

Around them sits Morpho, the progression layer — a hub, an in-game economy, daily challenges, seasons and leaderboards. You earn Goo, it fills a chrysalis, and the chrysalis becomes a butterfly in your trophy case. The nightly challenges are the part I am proudest of: step in anywhere, step in somewhere people already are, wave at someone new.
